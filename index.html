A SACCO owned by its members

Masaka City Busense Farmers SACCO is a member-owned savings and credit cooperative registered in Uganda under P.21653/RCS, operating from Kyanjale in Masaka City. We came together around a simple idea: when neighbours pool their savings, everyone can reach money when it matters, at a cost they can plan for.

Every member buys shares, which makes them a co-owner. The SACCO is not owned by an outside investor, so surplus is returned to members as dividends and the direction of the SACCO is decided at the general meeting.

From our counter in Kyanjale we serve farmers, market vendors, teachers, boda riders and small business owners with savings, loans, shares, mobile money, agent banking and everyday office services.

What we have achieved

From a small savings group to a registered SACCO serving Kyanjale and Masaka City.

    Started as a village savings and loan association (VSLA).

  2. July 2022

    Registered as a SACCO in Uganda under P.21653/RCS.

  3. 2024

    Held our Annual General Meeting, where members elected the board.

  4. Today

    Savings, loans, shares, mobile money, agent banking and stationery from our counter at Kyanjale.

Loan requirements

  1. Be a subscribed member
    You must be a subscribed member of the SACCO.
  2. Provide 2 guarantors
    Preferably a member and a spouse for couples.
  3. Attach National ID copies
    Clear copies for the member and the guarantors.
  4. Offer acceptable security
    Security or mortgage as per SACCO policy.
  5. Fill in the loan form
    Form fee is UGX 2,000.
